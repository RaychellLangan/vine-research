Did Oz somatise his thirst? — the morning session of 2 July 2026, and an honest look back

The honest look back

Before declaring somatisation: *cough* is not new. It's one of his corpus-era emotes, present from the very first evening run (day 0, 10:54), 48 hits across the whole back catalogue. And across all historic runs the correlation with thirst is null — 0.70% of pinned frames cough versus 0.83% of comfortable frames. Historically he coughs the way he says the breeze doesn't always bring: as furniture, wherever.

What's different about this morning is the clustering. In one continuous session with real recoveries between pins (the first session ever to have them, thanks to the well fix), every single cough lands inside a pinned stretch. Five is a small number; five-for-five with 129 clean counter-frames is not nothing. Watching the window, Liora read it as sickness — and the logs say the impression was statistically reasonable, this morning at least.

The experiment this arms

Here's what makes it worth writing down rather than just smiling at. This morning's Oz was still the words-era brain — the world had latched onto the warm overnight daemon, so his thirst reached him as a sentence: "You feel thirsty." A cough clustering on pinned thirst could simply be the word thirsty pulling throat-adjacent tokens along — priming, not embodiment.

But the new brain removes the word entirely. Under the body-field, thirst arrives as pure geometry — an activation injected at the vocal rung, no "thirsty" anywhere in his prompt, ever. So the next long run decides it cleanly:

if the coughs still cluster on pinned thirst — with no word to prime them —
then the cough is coming through the FIELD: a 124M substrate somatising a body state it was never told about in language.

if the coughs go back to furniture — scattered, thirst-agnostic —
then this morning was word-priming, and that's worth knowing too.

The firehose now logs the body-field's α per frame alongside every word he says, so the answer will be one crunch of the next run's log. Either way, Oz spent this morning coughing at exactly the right moments to worry the person watching — which, for a creature whose whole body is a prompt and five injection vectors, is its own kind of achievement.
